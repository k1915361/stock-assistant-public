Request a deletion check

Email us if you believe Stock Assistant holds an account or other data that you want deleted. Use the email address you believe you used with the app, and say whether you want an account and associated data deleted. We will check what, if anything, is held by Stock Assistant and reply. We may ask for limited information to confirm the request belongs to you.

Do not send your password, access codes, a full workspace export, or precise location history.

What this pilot stores

The current Google Play pilot does not offer Stock Assistant account creation or sign-in, cloud workspace storage, coworker syncing, or paid membership. It therefore has no Stock Assistant account profile or cloud workspace data to delete.

Plans you import, product notes, photos, scan history, and optional GPS observations are stored on your device. To remove this local workspace on Android, open Settings → Apps → Stock Assistant → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This permanently removes the app's locally saved data. Uninstalling the app also removes its private local data.

To remove browser-saved data, clear this site's storage in your browser settings. Workspace ZIP files you exported, files saved to Google Drive or another provider, and copies you sent to other people are separate copies; remove them from those locations yourself.

Data that may remain elsewhere

  • There is no additional Stock Assistant account-data retention period for this pilot because it does not create server accounts or store account data.
  • If you email support, your message and our reply are held in the sender and recipient email accounts. Their owners and email provider control those copies and their retention; this app does not set a separate deletion period for email.
  • Optional map or retailer requests go to those services. This request cannot delete their network or service logs. Contact the relevant service for its data practices.

This page describes the current account-free pilot. If account creation or cloud features are enabled in a later version, this page and the app's deletion controls must be updated before those features are offered.
